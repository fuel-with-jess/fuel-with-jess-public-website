FAQ

Common questions

What does a nutrition coach do?

A nutrition coach builds a personalized eating plan around your goals, training and lifestyle, then keeps you accountable with regular check-ins. At Fuel with Jess, that means macro targets, meal guidance and adjustments based on your progress, not a one-size-fits-all diet.

What's the difference between a nutrition coach and a registered dietitian?

A registered dietitian is a licensed clinician who can provide medical nutrition therapy for diagnosed conditions. A certified nutrition coach like Jess focuses on performance, body composition and healthy habits for generally healthy adults, and refers clients to a dietitian or doctor when medical care is needed.

How much does nutrition coaching cost?

Fuel with Jess coaching plans range from $149 to $349 per month, depending on check-in frequency and level of support. A 3-month commitment saves 15% ($127, $212 or $297 per month), then plans continue month to month. You can cancel with 30 days' written notice. You can also start with the one-time Nutrition Tip-Toe session for $179.

Do you offer virtual nutrition coaching?

Yes. Jess coaches clients virtually anywhere, and in person in San Diego. Virtual clients get the same plans, check-ins and support as local clients.

Can I eat carbs and still lose fat?

Yes. Fat loss comes from your overall energy balance, not from cutting out a food group, and carbs fuel strong training sessions. Jess builds plans that keep carbs in while you reach your body composition goals.

Do I have to count macros forever?

No. Tracking is a learning tool that teaches portions and balance. Most clients track closely at first, then shift to a more intuitive approach once the habits stick.

Is nutrition coaching HSA or FSA eligible?

It can be in some cases, usually when a doctor provides a Letter of Medical Necessity for a diagnosed condition. Check with your plan administrator before using HSA or FSA funds, and Jess can provide itemized receipts.

Do you offer corporate wellness workshops in San Diego?

Yes. Fuel with Jess delivers on-site and virtual nutrition workshops for San Diego companies, covering energy at work, meal prep and eating for performance. Jess brings 20 years of corporate leadership experience to every session.
