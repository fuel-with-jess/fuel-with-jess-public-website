FAQ

Common questions

Do you offer corporate wellness workshops in San Diego?

Yes. Fuel with Jess delivers on-site and virtual nutrition workshops for San Diego companies, covering energy at work, meal prep and eating for performance. Jess brings 20 years of corporate leadership experience to every session.

What's the difference between a nutrition coach and a registered dietitian?

A registered dietitian is a licensed clinician who can provide medical nutrition therapy for diagnosed conditions. A certified nutrition coach like Jess focuses on performance, body composition and healthy habits for generally healthy adults, and refers clients to a dietitian or doctor when medical care is needed.

Can I eat carbs and still lose fat?

Yes. Fat loss comes from your overall energy balance, not from cutting out a food group, and carbs fuel strong training sessions. Jess builds plans that keep carbs in while you reach your body composition goals.

Do I have to count macros forever?

No. Tracking is a learning tool that teaches portions and balance. Most clients track closely at first, then shift to a more intuitive approach once the habits stick.
